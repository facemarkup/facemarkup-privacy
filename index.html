FaceMarkup: Jumbo Drill
Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026. Applies to the Android app with package name com.facemarkup.app.

FaceMarkup works offline. It does not collect, send or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

What the app is

FaceMarkup is a tool for underground development jumbo operators. It helps mark up a face from survey offsets, record drilling rounds and headings, check a blast pattern, and look up rig reference material. It is not a survey instrument and does not replace survey.

Information the app stores

The app keeps the following on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • Face mark-up inputs: chainage, cut, face width, floor and lookout offsets, and the results.
  • Round and heading records you type in: heading names, chainages, hole counts, advance, ground notes.
  • Face photos you take or pick, stored as reduced-size copies inside the app.
  • Settings such as default face width and the last heading used.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.

Information the app does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or employee ID.
  • No location. The app does not use GPS or any location service.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage analytics.
  • No crash reports are sent to the developer.

Permissions

PermissionWhy it is declared
InternetDeclared by the app framework (Capacitor) so the built-in web view can run. The app makes no network requests. You can use it in flight mode and underground with no signal.
CameraNot requested. When you tap "Take photo" the app asks Android to open your normal camera app. Only the photo you take is handed back, and the full-size original is deleted after the app keeps its reduced copy.

Sharing and backups

Two features move data off the device, and both happen only when you start them:

  • Save backup writes a backup file to the Documents folder on your phone. It stays there until you delete it.
  • Send a copy opens the Android share sheet so you can send a backup or report to an app you pick, such as email or a messaging app. What happens to it after that is governed by the app you chose.

Deleting your data

Use Wipe data in the app's settings to erase all rounds, headings, photos and settings. Uninstalling the app also removes everything the app stored. Backup files you saved to Documents are ordinary files and are deleted by you.

Children

The app is a workplace tool for adults in mining. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking software. The 3D rig model and fonts are bundled inside the app and do not load from the internet.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date changed before that version is released.
